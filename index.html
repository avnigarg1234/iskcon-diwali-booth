Perspectives from History & Modern Innovation

Visionaries on Science, Change & Gita Wisdom

Great minds throughout history have turned to spiritual truth and mortality to understand existence, change, and purpose.

JO

J. Robert Oppenheimer

Theoretical Physicist & "Father of the Atomic Bomb"
"…Now I am become Death, the destroyer of worlds."

Context: Upon witnessing the atomic test at Trinity, Oppenheimer famously quoted the Bhagavad Gita (Chapter 11). He learned Sanskrit specifically to read the Gita in its original language.

📜 Inspired by Bhagavad Gita Ch. 11
SJ

Steve Jobs

Co-Founder of Apple Inc. & Visionary
"Death is very likely the single best invention of Life."

Context: From his famous 2005 Stanford address. Jobs spent months in India seeking spiritual awakening and kept Paramahansa Yogananda's spiritual writings as his lifelong guide, mirroring the Gita's teachings on impermanence (anityam) and fearless purpose.

Modern Dilemmas & Ancient Wisdom

Navigating the AI Wave

An Identity Crisis & A Timeless Spiritual Solution

As Artificial Intelligence transforms our workplaces, schools, and daily routines at unprecedented speeds, humanity faces a profound question: Who am I when machines can think and calculate faster than me?

⚓ Gita Wisdom for the AI Era (BG 2.14)

"Happiness and distress, success and technological disruption are non-permanent—like summer and winter seasons. One who learns to tolerate them with steady spiritual clarity is a true yogi."

By grounding ourselves and our children in Bhagavad Gita's science of consciousness, we navigate AI advancements with emotional stability, clarity of purpose, and inner peace.
