What is ISKCON, in plain words?

ISKCON (the International Society for Krishna Consciousness), often called the Hare Krishna movement, is a spiritual community founded in 1966. Its teachings come from the Bhagavad Gita, an ancient Indian text about who we are and how to live with purpose and peace.

Modern dilemmas & ancient wisdom

Navigating the AI wave

An identity question, and a timeless answer

AI is changing how we work, learn, and even what we think makes us valuable. If machines can calculate and create faster than us, it's fair to ask: who am I, then?

⚓ Gita wisdom for the AI era · BG 2.14

Joy and distress, gain and loss, come and go like summer and winter. The wise learn to bear them calmly instead of being thrown around by them.

Your worth isn't your output.
The Gita places identity in consciousness, not in skills a machine might copy.
Stay steady through change.
Technology shifts fast. Inner stability doesn't have to.
Use tools, don't be used.
Purpose and values guide how we use technology, not the other way around.

Inquiry & practical solutions

Questions that touch the heart

Tap any question for a short answer from the Gita's point of view. It's a starting point, not the whole story.

01Who am I, and what is life for?Self-realization · Gita Ch. 2

The Gita teaches that you are more than your body, job title, or achievements. You are a conscious, eternal self, and the body is like a vehicle you are traveling in.

From this view, life's purpose is to rediscover that deeper self and its natural joy and connection, rather than only chasing the next goal.

02Is life just aging, sickness, and loss?Beyond material ups and downs

Change is certain: bodies age, situations shift, people come and go. The Gita points to something steadier, the self that observes all these changes without being destroyed by them.

That doesn't remove pain, but it offers a foundation of hope and perspective when things feel unstable.

03Can an ancient book help with modern problems?Practical work & mind mastery

The Gita is set on a battlefield, in the middle of a stressful decision. Its advice is about acting well under pressure, staying steady in success and failure, and working without being consumed by anxiety.

People apply it to careers, relationships, school stress, and finding balance.

04Do I have to be Hindu, or religious, to join in?Everyone is welcome

No. People of every background, belief, and level of curiosity are welcome, including skeptics. You can simply come, listen, ask questions, and decide for yourself.

Try it tonight

Anxiety & stress relief through mantra meditation

Mantra meditation uses sound to quiet a busy mind. It's simple, free, and good for adults, teens, and kids alike.

🔔 A 2-minute practice

The Hare Krishna mantra

Hare Krishna, Hare Krishna,
Krishna Krishna, Hare Hare
Hare Rama, Hare Rama,
Rama Rama, Hare Hare

  1. Sit comfortably and take a few slow breaths.
  2. Repeat the mantra softly out loud or quietly in your mind.
  3. When your mind wanders (it will), gently bring your attention back to the sound.

About Diwali

Diwali, the festival of lights, celebrates the victory of light over darkness and good over evil. Many devotees remember the return of Lord Rama to Ayodhya, welcomed home by rows of glowing lamps. Whatever your background, we hope the light of today brightens your year.
